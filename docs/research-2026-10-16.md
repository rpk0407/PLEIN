### C. Hype and marketing that work, and what is legal in France

**What creates hype in 2026 (costs where verified):**
- **DexScreener.** Enhanced Token Info costs **$299** (list price $499), processed in under 15 minutes. That buys the logo, socials and description, and is basically mandatory on day 1. Banner ads start at $299 and a trending-bar slot at **$2,000**. Boosts are bought from the token page in 12–24 h packs, and 500 active boosts turn the ticker gold. - **Native formats that work and are free:** a live X Space at launch ("le plein en direct"), the weekly receipt ritual, the meme generator plus a free-entry contest, collaborations with other community coins (co-memes, not paid shills), and IRL stunts that are legal and non-political. For example: a "ticket de caisse géant" photo outside a food bank when handing over the donation, with the association's consent.
- **Cost of automated posting on X.** X API is pay-per-use since 6 Feb 2026: **$0.015 per post, $0.20 per post containing a URL** (since 20 Apr 2026), $0.005 per post read, with reads capped at 3M a month. So automated posts should carry no links (put the link in the bio or pinned post). That makes 100 bot posts a month cost $1.50 instead of $20.

**The French and EU legal limits (the binding constraints):**
- **Loi n° 2023-451, art. 4 V.** For anyone who promotes "à titre onéreux" (for money, tokens, commissions or benefits in kind), it bans promotion of crypto-asset services unless the advertiser is MiCA-authorised. It also bans promotion of crypto-assets themselves unless they are linked to such an authorised provider. Since **1 July 2026, PSAN registration alone no longer qualifies**. Reported penalties go up to **2 years' imprisonment and €300,000**, with the influencer jointly liable. Enforcement is real. A joint AMF–DGCCRF press release of 30 Dec 2025 says AMF monitoring identified more than 150 "fininfluenceurs" with 10,000+ followers each, about 30 of them under particular attention, and that in 2024–2025 the DGCCRF ordered around twenty influencers to stop, notably over crypto-assets. The ACPR-AMF joint hub's 2025 annual report (page updated 24 Sep 2026) adds that 14 influencer promotions were sent to the DGCCRF and 1,300+ unauthorised sites were blacklisted in 2025. **Consequences for $PLEIN:** (1) never pay or gift tokens to French creators to promote it; (2) don't run "shill-to-earn" bounties; (3) don't promote trading-bot referral links for commission to a French audience, since those are crypto services without MiCA authorisation; (4) unpaid organic opinions are outside this law but remain subject to financial law.
- **MiCA Title II** has applied since 30 Dec 2024 to offerors and persons seeking admission to trading of "other crypto-assets": a white paper (Art 6), notification (Art 8), marketing rules (Art 7), publication (Art 9) and conduct rules (Art 14). Art 14 requires acting honestly and fairly, communicating "in a fair, clear and not misleading manner", acting in holders' best interests and treating them equally, unless preferential treatment is disclosed. Art 7 requires marketing to be clearly identifiable, consistent with the white paper, and published only *after* the white paper where one is required. Exemptions exist (e.g. offers to fewer than 150 persons per Member State, or small offers under the €1M threshold). Whether a fair launch on a bonding curve makes the founders "offerors" is a **genuine grey zone. Get one hour of French crypto counsel before launch.** At minimum, write a short "fiche $PLEIN" in white-paper style and model all marketing on Art 7/14 standards: no promises of gains; "buyback" described as a mechanism, not a return; the founders' 43% clearly disclosed.
- **Contests and giveaways.** Lotteries are banned by default (Code de la sécurité intérieure L322-1). Promotional draws are allowed under the Code de la consommation if they are not unfair. Since 2011 a promotional draw *can* be limited to customers, but the purchase must be of the product itself, never of the right to play. Requiring people to buy a speculative token to enter a draw risks being treated as an illegal paid lottery under the ANJ's remit. **Run only free-entry, skill-based meme contests judged by a jury**, with written rules and adults only.
- **Political and brand hygiene.** Don't use union, party or movement logos. Don't use real people's images. Don't use the TotalEnergies or Leclerc marks, or brand colours applied to a pump. Say plainly on the site: « $PLEIN ne représente aucun mouvement, parti, syndicat ni marque. »
- **Gaps:** I did not obtain the primary text of the ARPP rules or the current X and Telegram crypto-ad policies. Check them before running any *paid* ad. The recommended plan avoids paid ads anyway.

### D. Automation stack (Solana, 2026)

**1. Claiming creator fees: an important nuance.**
- Jupiter's docs confirm a **1% fee on every buy and sell, split 50% creator / 50% Jupiter, before and after graduation** to Meteora DAMM v2.
- Swarms, which moved off Studio, reports that **Jupiter Studio paid creators from its own fee-sharing vault, so the creator share on-chain showed as 0%**. Claims went through Studio's Fees tab. So on a Studio launch, `client.creator.claimCreatorTradingFee` (Meteora DBC SDK `@meteora-ag/dynamic-bonding-curve-sdk`) may not be the right path. Check this on devnet or with a tiny test launch **before Saturday**.
- If you launch natively on Meteora DBC instead, the SDK exposes `claimCreatorTradingFee`, `claimCreatorTradingFeeToReceiver` (explicit receiver, e.g. your Squads vault) and `claimCreatorTradingFee2` (transfer-hook pools, SDK 1.5.8+). After graduation, `claimPositionFee` claims the DAMM v2 locked-LP fees. Open-source launchpads pack both into "claim all" transactions of 1,232 bytes or less.
- Some DBC integrators report the creator nets about 0.4% after Meteora's protocol share. Model revenue at 0.4–0.5% of volume.

**2. Buybacks.**
- Use **Jupiter Swap API V2** (`https://api.jup.ag/swap/v2`). The older Ultra API is "no longer actively maintained". V2 offers a Meta-Aggregator path (assembled transaction, managed landing) and a Router path (raw instructions, for building claim → swap → burn in one transaction).
- For a smoother price impact, split the weekly buyback into slices, e.g. 12 swaps over 60 minutes after 18:00. Jupiter's Recurring (DCA) product is the alternative; I did not verify its current API terms.
- Burn with the SPL Token `burn` instruction from the executor wallet, never by sending to a "dead" address. Then publish the transaction signature.

**3. Treasury: Squads v4.**
- Supports thresholds, **time locks, spending limits, roles and sub-accounts**, and is audited by Neodyme and OtterSec (SDK `@sqds/multisig`).
- Setup: a 2-of-2 founder vault (plus a third recovery key held offline) receives all claimed fees.
- Grant one **bot member a weekly spending limit** (the CLI command `AddSpendingLimit &lt;mint&gt; &lt;amount&gt; &lt;period&gt; &lt;members&gt; &lt;destinations&gt;` restricts destinations). It lets the bot move only the computed buyback and food-aid amounts to the executor and association addresses.
- Put a 24–48 h **time lock** on config changes.
- In the Squads app, spending limits are a Squads Pro feature; via the SDK or CLI they are part of the protocol.

**4. Data feeds.**
- **Fuel, instant:** `prix-des-carburants-en-france-flux-instantane-v2` on data.economie.gouv.fr (API, CSV/JSON). Updated every 10 minutes, **Licence Ouverte / Open Licence 2.0**, which allows free commercial reuse with attribution ("Source : Ministère de l'Économie – prix-carburants.gouv.fr").
- **Fuel, official weekly:** the DGEC database on ecologie.gouv.fr is **published every Monday with the previous Friday's average prices**, with a weekly PDF note (e.g. "NPG-2026.10.02"). **Fix the formula:** a Saturday 18:00 buyback must use the DGEC figure published the preceding Monday, i.e. the price from 8 days earlier. Say so explicitly on the site, and show the live feed alongside it for atmosphere.
- **Markets:** the DexScreener API is free with no key (300 requests/min on pair endpoints, 60/min on promo endpoints). Use Helius webhooks or enhanced transactions for the live buy ticker and holder count (I did not verify current Helius pricing). SOL OS / rhc-engine handles bundle, wallet-farm and wash-trading detection.

**5. The weekly job (GitHub Actions cron or Vercel Cron; idempotent; dry-run on Friday):**
1. 17:45: fetch the DGEC weekly diesel price, compute `share = clamp(price×20, 30, 60)`, and store a signed snapshot.
2. 17:50: claim fees into the Squads vault.
3. 18:00: execute the spending-limit transfers to the executor (buyback) and the association.
4. 18:00–19:00: sliced swaps USDC→PLEIN, then burn.
5. 19:05: render a receipt PNG (Satori/`@vercel/og` or Playwright), post it to X (no link, $0.015) and to Telegram (Bot API), and append it to the site archive.
6. Alerting: if any step fails, page both founders. Never retry with a larger amount.

**6. Site revenue from swaps.**
- The Jupiter Plugin accepts `referralAccount` + `referralFee` (bps). Under Ultra, Jupiter takes **20% of integrator fees**, fees require Referral Program token accounts per fee mint, and routing is forced to Metis.
- Example: a 50 bps referral fee nets 40 bps to you. On $100K of swaps through the widget, that is $400.
- **Recommendation:** set it to 0–25 bps and send 100% of it to the réservoir. A visible "0% site fee" may convert better than the revenue it costs.

**7. Merch.** Print-on-demand (Printful/Printify via Shopify) with crypto checkout (Solana Pay, Helio/MoonPay Commerce). Designs: the receipt tee, the "RACHAT 46,6 %" totem hoodie, a road-sign sticker pack. I did not verify current POD or checkout fees.

### E. The "perpetual money engine": what the evidence says

**Proven reference points:**

Allium Labs data cited by the Financial Times (via Cointelegraph, 31 Aug 2026) credits Pump.fun with nearly $200M of 2026 buybacks. - **Hyperliquid.** About $370M of buybacks in 2026. Together with Pump.fun that is about 90% of the record $638M in token buybacks Allium tracked through late August, up from $545M in the same period of 2025.

GMGN pays 10–30% by referred volume, on a 1% trading fee. **Legal problem:** promoting them for commission to a French audience falls under art. 4 V. **Engine design: route external money into the réservoir.**
1. **SOL OS "Contrôle technique" as a service.** Other Solana and Robinhood Chain launchers buy a live, embeddable bundle/farm/wash-trading audit badge for their site, for example a monthly USDC subscription. **A fixed 25% of gross goes to the $PLEIN réservoir.** This is real external revenue, and it is the only moat nobody else can copy.
2. **The fuel utility as a media asset.** The "Mon Plein" tool can earn non-crypto revenue: sponsorships from fuel-saving or EV-adjacent brands, and affiliate deals on car insurance or eco-driving products. Keep this revenue strictly separate from token promotion, with a share sent to the réservoir.
3. **Merch.** 100% of the margin goes to the réservoir; receipt tees can show the week's buyback.
4. **Widget referral fee** (if any): 100% to the réservoir.
5. **Idle USDC yield** (Kamino, Jupiter Lend): only for a small, multisig-held buffer, never the weekly buyback pot. The risks are smart-contract failure, depeg and withdrawal delays, and the upside on small balances is trivial.

Holders then benefit from money that does **not** come from their own trading. That is the only form of "perpetual" that holds up to scrutiny, and it should be described as a mechanism, never as a promised return.

**Failure modes:** buybacks smaller than founder selling, hidden team wallets, changing the split after launch (the BONK pattern), "revenue" that is just wash trading, and AI-agent tokens with no paying customers.

### F. Risks and failure modes

- **Copycats and impersonation.** Expect fake $PLEIN tokens within minutes, and fake "support" bots in Telegram replies. Countermeasures: show the contract address in every channel, pin it in the X bio, run a Telegram with admin-only DMs disabled, publish a "nous ne vous écrirons jamais en premier" rule, and add a site "vérifier un contrat" box powered by SOL OS.
- **X account suspension.** Use a dedicated brand account, warmed up and verified; no follow/like automation; stay well inside rate limits; keep a backup account and a Telegram channel. Keep the founder's personal account out of it entirely, given his CAIO role.
- **Regulatory.** French enforcement against finfluencers is active (see section C). The biggest self-inflicted risk is paying people to promote. The second is promising gains or framing the coin politically around 17 October.
- **Timing optics.** Launching at 18:00 on a day of protest over the cost of living invites the accusation of exploiting anger. Mitigate with the food-aid receipt, the free fuel tool, and strict neutrality.
- **Tax (France, 2026).**
  - Individuals: crypto gains on disposal are taxed under the **31.4% flat tax (12.8% income tax + 18.6% social contributions)**, effective 1 January 2026. There is a €305 annual exemption, and crypto-to-crypto swaps remain non-taxable events.
  - DAC8 platform reporting started in January 2026.
